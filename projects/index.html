projects

Ongoing and completed research projects on safety-critical control, predictive frameworks, and autonomous systems.

Subspace Shaping & Conditioning in Data-Driven MPC

Developing numerically stable, constraint-compliant, and reproducible subspace shaping frameworks for data-driven predictive flight control.

Model-Based Loosely Coupled GNSS/INS Integration

Model-based loosely coupled Kalman filter development in MATLAB/Simulink for tactical and MALE UAV navigation and flight control computers.

Real-Time Algebraic MPC for Terrain Following in High-Subsonic UAVs

Real-time altitude reference generation and terrain following via Algebraic Model Predictive Control, online Digital Elevation Models (DEM), and HIL validation.
