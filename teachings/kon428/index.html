## Course Overview This course provides a comprehensive introduction to safety-critical control architectures in railway engineering, train protection systems (ATP/ATO/ATS), and fail-safe interlocking logic design. - Learn vital logic design and railway interlocking principles - Understand automatic train control frameworks (ETCS / CBTC) - Compare fixed block vs. moving block signaling architectures - Apply Safety Integrity Level (SIL) standards to railway control design ## Prerequisites - Control Systems Theory - Logic Design Fundamentals ## Textbooks & References - "Railway Signalling and Interlocking" by Gregor Theiss - "Principles of Railway Location and Design" by Siripreet Singh ## Catalog & Links - **ITU OBS Official Page:** [KON 428 Course Catalog](https://obs.itu.edu.tr/public/DersKatalog/DersKatalogBilgiBransDersKodu?bransKodu=KON&dersNo=428)
