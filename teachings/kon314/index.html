## Course Overview This course covers the dynamic modeling, analysis, and feedback control system design principles for industrial processes. Students gain practical insights into controller tuning and stability criteria for single-input single-output (SISO) control loops. - Understand end-to-end process dynamics and transfer function derivation - Analyze transient and steady-state responses of first and second-order systems - Design and tune PID controllers using classical methods - Implement feedforward, cascade, and ratio control strategies ## Prerequisites - Differential Equations - Basics of Linear Systems Theory ## Textbooks & References - "Process Dynamics and Control" by Dale E. Seborg, Thomas F. Edgar, Duncan A. Mellichamp, Francis J. Doyle III - "Feedback Control of Dynamic Systems" by Gene F. Franklin, J. David Powell, Abbas Emami-Naeini ## Catalog & Links - **ITU OBS Official Page:** [KON 314 Course Catalog](https://obs.itu.edu.tr/public/DersKatalog/DersKatalogBilgiBransDersKodu?bransKodu=KON&dersNo=314)
