## Course Overview This course covers the dynamic modeling, analysis, and feedback control system design principles for industrial processes. Students gain practical insights into controller tuning and stability criteria for single-input single-output (SISO) control loops. ## Prerequisites - Differential Equations - Basics of Linear Systems Theory ## Catalog & Links - **ITU OBS Official Course Page:** [KON 314 Course Information](https://obs.itu.edu.tr/public/DersKatalog/DersKatalogBilgiBransDersKodu?bransKodu=KON&dersNo=314)
